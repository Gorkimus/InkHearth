Since the last letter: a nudge for every queue, a spoiler rule that finally speaks plainly, one search box for every book — and now, a way to call someone over by name.

📣 Say their name 5.22 · Oct 16

  • Tag your hearth. Type @ while chatting about a book and the member list appears — pick a name and it lands in your comment as a highlighted tag. Whoever you tagged gets one pointed ring on the bell, and the comment waits at the top of their Chatter by the Fire card until they've actually laid eyes on it.
  • The gate keeps its word. Tag someone in a spoiler from ahead of where they are and the bell still rings — but the comment stays locked until they catch up, same as every spoiler here.

🔍 One box for every book 5.21 · Oct 16

  • A 🔍 on the nav. Search consults the same catalogs as Log a book, then says who here already has it. Clicking a result opens your own copy, a member's shelf with their notes and the chatter, or the catalog card with a "log it yourself" link. Tick in the hearth to search only the household's shelves.
  • Every author and series is a doorway. Names in the book panels are now links that fill this same search — and the catalog panel and members' shelves grew "Add to library" and "Add to TBR" buttons for anything not on your shelf yet.

🔥 Speak freely 5.20 · Oct 16

  • Spoilers gate, everything else shows. A comment is held back only if it's marked a spoiler and written from ahead of where you are — a spoiler posted at 75% appears once you reach 75%. Plain comments show no matter how far ahead their author was.
  • The fire keeps the whole conversation. Chatter by the Fire pages back through the household's talk ten comments at a time, with a quiet count of the spoilers still waiting for you.

👍 A nudge for the queue 5.19 · Oct 16

  • Bump a book up someone's TBR. Every profile gains a "Their TBR 👍" view — each queued book wearing a thumbs-up you can give or take back. Bumps count on their queue and ring their bell once: read this next.
