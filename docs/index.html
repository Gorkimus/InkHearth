✨ for you

Recommendations that know your tiers

It begins with a taste digest — your tiers, your series, your DNFs, the narrators you love — carried to an LLM with a simple charge: propose something worth your time. Every candidate is verified against Hardcover before it reaches your screen, so only real books make the journey. Dials for adventure, length, series vs standalones and count; a quiet "not for me" teaches future runs. Keys stay flexible: the household may share one server-wide key, or each member brings their own — a free Google AI Studio key works, and yours outranks the house.

🕯️ light your own

Self-hosting takes a minute

Any machine with Docker becomes your reading hearth. First boot prints a one-time claim link — open it, name yourself, done. Then invite the household.

# on whatever machine hums quietly in your corner
git clone https://github.com/Gorkimus/InkHearth.git
cd InkHearth
docker compose up -d --build    # → http://localhost:3222
COOKIE_SECUREset to 1 when serving over https (tunnel / reverse proxy)
TRUST_PROXY1 behind a reverse proxy, so rate limits see real client IPs
HARDCOVER_TOKENfree hardcover.app token — fills pages, runtimes, series, genres
LLM_API_KEYoptional server-wide key for taste-matched recommendations
KOBO_SYNC_HOURShow often to pull Kobo progress (0 = manual "Sync now")
