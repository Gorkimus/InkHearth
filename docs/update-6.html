UPDATE 6

🔥 InkHearth — "The Streak and the Shelf"

September 25, 2026 · already live — refresh your tab and you're on the latest

Since the last letter: your hearth learned to keep count of the days you showed up, your shelf learned to point at the next book worth reading, and the search box learned the difference between a whisper and a quote.

🔥 Reading streaks

  • The top of the Hearth now keeps a streak — a count of consecutive days where you logged any reading progress. Finishing a book counts. Adding one counts. Even the smallest nudge forward counts (more on that below). Miss a day and the fire dims; come back and you're lighting it again.
  • Your best run ever is kept alongside the current one — best 14 · today makes 6 — because a record is its own quiet encouragement.
  • Streaks show up in Reading together too: beside every friend's in-progress book you'll see their 🔥 count, so you can tell who's on a tear and who could use a nudge.

🎚️ Fine-grained progress — the ±1% buttons

  • Between −10% and +10% on your reading card sit two new buttons: −1% and +1%. Read three pages before bed? Tap once. The bar, the estimate, and your streak all notice.
  • Progress saved through the book panel's edit form counts toward your streak as well — anywhere you tell the truth about where you are, the hearth hears it.

📚 The Series journey, rebuilt

  • The Series journey card on the Hearth now leads with the series you've most recently finished a book from — five of them, newest first, with a See all link when the shelf runs deeper.
  • Every series title is clickable and lands you in Log-a-book with that series already loaded, in reading order — checking what comes next takes one tap.
  • The counts tell the truth now: "4 of 9" means you've finished four of nine, and the next suggestion always skips anything already on your shelf — what it offers is a book you can actually go get.
  • The series lists themselves got a deep clean: translations under other narrators' names, alternate editions, prologue re-issues and store splits have been gathered out of the way. When it says a series has nine books, there are nine books.

🔍 Search that follows your intent

  • Quotes mean exact. Search "the hunger games" and you get The Hunger Games — the whole phrase, nothing that merely mentions a word from it.
  • Clicks are exact too. Tapping an author or series name anywhere in the app takes you to that author, that series — the loose, forgiving matching stays available for free-typed wandering.
  • A new "in library" toggle shows books you already own alongside the results, and both it and collections & samples remember how you left them.
  • The Log-a-book confirm panel gained a More info expander: the description, page and audio lengths, and one-tap links to Hardcover, Goodreads and Open Library — handy for vetting a book before it earns a shelf spot.

🪵 While the kettle was on

  • Mid-progress entries read like proper English now — listening in progress, reading in progress — small thing, but you look at it every day.
  • The −10% button found its undo partner: deleting an entry offers a six-second Undo, and the reading card refreshes in place so tap-tap-tapping through a chapter never blinks the page.
  • The book panel stays open while you work in it — quick actions update it where it stands instead of closing and reopening the world.
  • Warm light by default: Warm and Classic themes, switchable under Account → Style, and the nav's home is called The Hearth.
  • Under the floorboards: phantom finishes from device syncs were chased out, and series recommendations keep to first-in-series you haven't read.
💬 Try this tonight: open the Hearth, tap +1% on whatever you're mid-way through, and watch the streak catch. Then click a series on the Series journey and see where the shelf points you.
